## Problem
People often rely on multiple websites and applications to manage different parts of their fitness journey. One app provides workout plans, another tracks progress, another offers nutrition guidance, while coaching and educational resources are found elsewhere. This fragmented experience makes it difficult to stay organized and consistent.

## Solution

HealthApp brings these essential fitness services together in one platform, allowing users to manage their entire fitness journey from a single application.

## Features
- Client registration
- Coach registration
- REST APIs
- Workout planning (Coming soon)
- Nutrition planning (Coming soon)
